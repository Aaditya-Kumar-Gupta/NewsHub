Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. This policy explains what NewsHub collects and why.

What we store

Your name, email, and a securely hashed password. We never store your password in plain text. We also store the interests, sources, and city/state/country you choose during onboarding.

Location data

We only store city-level location — never your exact device GPS coordinates — even when you use "current location," which is snapped to roughly 1km precision before it's saved.

Reading activity

We keep a record of articles you've read and saved so we can show you your history and power your personalized feed. You can clear this at any time from your profile.

Third parties

Headlines are retrieved from NewsAPI.org through our own servers — your browser never contacts NewsAPI directly, and your account credentials are never shared with it.

Your controls

You can update your interests, location, and theme at any time, and you can permanently delete your account and all associated data from the Profile page.
