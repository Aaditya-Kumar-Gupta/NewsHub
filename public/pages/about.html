About NewsHub

NewsHub is a personalized, location-aware news reader. We believe a good front page should reflect two things: what you actually care about, and where you actually live — not just what's loudest that day.

How personalization works

Every story in your feed is scored using a transparent, rule-based system: interest match (40%), location relevance (25%), recency (15%), source preference (10%), and a trending signal (10%). No black-box models — just a formula you could compute yourself.

Where the news comes from

Headlines and search results are sourced through NewsAPI.org and a broad network of publishers, then normalized and indexed by NewsHub's own backend before they ever reach your feed.

Our principles

We store the minimum location data needed to localize your feed — typically just your city, state, and country — and we never sell your reading history.
